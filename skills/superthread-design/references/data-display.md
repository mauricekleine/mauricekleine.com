# Data display

IDs, times, code and any figure that must hold a column set in Fragment Mono, because Supreme and Erode have no tabular figures, and numbers in a table align right. A table row is 48 px, and the board's row is 56 px.

## Zero, missing and comparisons (F14)

Show a real zero as zero and a missing value as missing. When a comparison starts from zero, use a count or plain explanation instead of a percentage growth badge. Neutral or undefined change uses the neutral treatment (the queued ink and its dash glyph), without implying success or failure. A state is one of the five statuses, shown as a badge with its glyph and its word.

## Chart legends (F15)

Derive chart marks and their legend swatches from the same style definition. A chart series is a ship's series: it prints in the ship's spot through its line screen (`texture-line-<ship>`), whose angle keeps the series apart without colour. `--chart-1` to `--chart-5` carry five of the spots in chart order: orbit, constellation, quanta, artifacts, trisys. The swatch repeats the plotted mark's colour, screen and outline. Include only series present in the view. The system gives colour to ship series only, so ask before giving another kind of series a spot.
