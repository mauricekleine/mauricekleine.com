# superthread.mauricekleine.com: the design system site

Scope: the site's overview and every page under it (foundations, components, mock pages, install). Visitor mode: Read, with Operate mock pages inside it.

- Audience: Maurice, and the agents that build his apps.
- Job: see every token and component of superthread in use, on a docs page, a working board and a reading page, and trust it holds across all three.
- Action: install it with one command (`bunx --bun shadcn@latest add @mk/tokens @mk/button`), or open the page for the part being built.
- Proof: the mock pages are real compositions from the registry's own components, in light and dark.
- Constraints: the four pinned faces from one delivery, light and dark from one set of tokens, phone to wide desktop, lowercase and understated words.

Chosen direction: the master, Maurice's own remix of the direction round (scratch `week1/i1/MASTER-BRIEF.md`, built in `worlds/master/`). Memorable moment: the cropped opener band, a thread screened in key and plum, with the wordmark set over it.

Unresolved: the custom domain and its Workers Builds project wait for Maurice's go-ahead.

## Direction contract

THESIS: superthread is a printed proof sheet for the fleet: two inks on white stock, exact and spacious, with texture only where you look. It refuses the stock shadcn docs shell (rounded cards, grey sidebar, gradient accents) and neon dev-tool dark.

OWN-WORLD: key ink #16181d and its wedge of tints on white; one house ink, plum (#aa337a, #c074a1 in dark); square corners; 1 px rules that run to both window edges; Unbounded display, Supreme interface, Erode reading, Fragment Mono labels; halftone and dither in five places only (opener, figures, marks, state fields, empty and loading), framed by corner crop marks; statuses as dusty inks with a glyph square; 1-bit ship marks with a dithered spot; a raked shadow under sheets raised on the deep ground.

STORY: the visitor understands what superthread is, sees it hold on docs, a board and a reading page, and installs it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: masthead cells across the top (mark and wordmark, breadcrumb and search, system, version and date, the house swatch); left nav with the boxed current item and the light, dark and auto control at its foot; centre, the cropped opener with the Unbounded wordmark, one Erode sentence, the install patch, the six-ship fleet row and the plum primary action; right, on this page, the wedge and the states. Signature interaction: the fleet rail's label slides out as a tab on hover and focus. Motion: 120, 180 and 240 ms on one ease-out curve; reduced motion keeps every state and drops the travel.

FORM: the exposure record's zone sheets carrying the two-ink proof's screens; first on the ordered list as Maurice's remix; seed:9553ea8ea3c775a6

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
